### How It Works (Application & Learning Journey)
1. **Apply & Enroll**: Select your preferred domain and complete the enrollment process to secure your spot.
2. **Access the Dashboard**: Log in to the student dashboard to access your offer letter, weekly tasks, and learning resources.
3. **Build Projects**: Complete weekly tasks and build real-world projects with mentor guidance.
4. **Get Certified**: Submit your final evaluation to receive your verified internship completion certificate and recommendation letter.

### Certificate & Evaluation Policy
- Every completion certificate includes a unique Verification ID.
- Verification URL: `https://internnova.co.in/verify`
- Certificate View URL: `https://internnova.co.in/certificate/{cert_id}`
- Evaluation includes project submission and mentor review before issuance.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
1. **Do I need prior coding experience?**
   - *Answer*: Basic understanding helps, but our programs are designed to take you from fundamentals to advanced concepts through practical projects.
2. **Will I get a verified certificate?**
   - *Answer*: Yes, every completion certificate comes with a unique Verification ID that employers can check directly on our platform.
3. **Are the internships paid?**
   - *Answer*: The core learning internships are unpaid skill-building programs. However, top performers may receive paid freelance opportunities or stipends based on their project performance.
4. **How does the placement support work?**
   - *Answer*: For Pro Internships, we help you build an ATS-friendly resume, optimize your portfolio, conduct mock interviews, and share your profile with our hiring partners.
